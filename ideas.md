# Design Exploration: Email Marketing Review Miner

## Three Directions Considered

### 1. Signal Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A research desk that feels like a meticulously annotated field report: calm mineral surfaces, decisive data marks, and editorial hierarchy. It makes competitive feedback feel traceable rather than abstract.

**Probability:** 0.06

### 2. Orchard Metrics
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, optimistic planning studio inspired by horticultural diagrams and growth cycles. Pain points become pruning opportunities, with lively greens and tactile paper cues.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Night Relay
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast operations console with deep ink backgrounds and precise alert colors. It foregrounds speed and priority as if the strategy team is monitoring a live signal feed.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Signal Ledger

### Design Movement
**Editorial data journalism** informed by Swiss information design and the tactility of a premium research notebook. The work should read as evidence-led strategy rather than a generic dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence first:** Sources, sample scope, and methodological limits are visible—not relegated to fine print.
2. **Asymmetric clarity:** A persistent analytical rail and offset content columns create the sensation of reading an annotated dossier.
3. **Purposeful color:** One prominent coral signal color identifies problems and opportunities; all other hues serve reading and comparison.
4. **Tactile restraint:** Paper warmth, hairline rules, and subtle halftone texture add character without diluting data precision.

### Color Philosophy
The base is **warm manuscript paper** and dark graphite: comfortable for extended reading, suitable for executive work, and deliberately unlike the blue/white SaaS dashboard default. A single saturated **signal coral** identifies priority friction, complemented by a mineral teal that identifies positive sentiment. Muted saffron indicates watch-list concern, preserving a hierarchy of urgency without visual noise.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is composed as an **annotated long-form research dossier**. A fixed left provenance rail holds the report identity, scope, and navigation. The main story moves through varied editorial blocks: an opening finding band, a horizontal competitor ledger, a landscape evidence matrix, then a recommendation sequence. No centered hero-card stack is used.

### Signature Elements
1. A vertical **provenance rail** with report metadata, active section tracking, and the mark.
2. **Margin annotations**—small source tags, sample counts, and concise evidence labels set apart from primary copy.
3. A recurring **signal slash** (coral diagonal rule) that anchors priority callouts, chart selections, and the logo motif.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions clarify evidence instead of decorating it. Hovering a competitor emphasizes its row across related charts; filtering pain points updates the finding and recommended response. Download and share use clear, utility-oriented controls. Navigation scrolls to dossier chapters with instant, legible feedback.

### Animation
At page arrival, ledger rows and annotations reveal in a 40–70ms stagger using opacity and a 10px upward transform. Charts use a brief 240ms ease-out entrance and respond to hover with color/opacity changes only. Buttons press to 0.97 scale over 140ms. Motion is disabled under reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Fraunces** is reserved for consequential findings and product-strategy statements: slightly human, deliberate, and credible. **DM Sans** carries interface labels, metadata, chart keys, and body copy for technical legibility. Headlines use a compact Fraunces display scale; body copy is 15–17px DM Sans; labels are uppercase, letter-spaced, and compact.

### Brand Essence
**Signal Ledger is a review-intelligence dossier for product teams that need defensible competitive whitespace, not another generic sentiment score.**

Personality: **Forensic, decisive, humane.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, evidence-literate, and framed as implications. CTAs are utilitarian and specific. Microcopy acknowledges scope rather than overselling certainty.

Example lines:
- “Pricing friction is not a billing problem. It is a retention signal.”
- “Trace the complaint. Build the escape hatch.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is a bold, text-free **split ledger mark**: two stacked graphite bars cut by a single coral diagonal signal line. The wordmark uses custom-spaced DM Sans capitals, never a default font treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Coral — #EF665B.** A clear, ownable intervention color used only where evidence turns into a decision.

## Style Decisions

- The provenance rail is paired with a full-width, readable editorial column and landscape evidence fields; asymmetry must never collapse the dossier into a narrow strip.
- Quiet page areas operate as intentional manuscript margins, carrying source labels, sample context, and restrained rules rather than unassigned blank space.
- Fraunces findings use purposeful, compact line composition while DM Sans body and research metadata retain comfortable report-scale measures.
- The coral diagonal signal recurs at decision moments—hero, thesis callout, chart selection, and the split-ledger mark—while remaining sparse enough to retain authority.
